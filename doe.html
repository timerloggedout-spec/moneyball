The methods of this epoch

Record: docs/ops/DOE-METHODS-EPOCH-2026-11-03.md (termux-monorepo; PRs #4722 · #4724 · #4725 · #4727 · #4731) — the experiment methods actually implemented this epoch, from receipts, plus the cycle as practiced. MVT (separation seat) concurred as co-source.
  1. Replay-audit invariants. The settlement machinery is audited by re-running its own replay against invariants B1–B5 + F1, with deliberately broken fixtures exercised: a check that cannot fail proves nothing. The audit's control case: a forecast of 4/5 on a settled YES scores Brier 1/25 — arithmetic the proof packet later discharges by computation.
  2. Two-leg corroboration census. A claim counts as corroborated only when at least two independent legs agree; a single-leg mark is held out of weighting and flagged; disputed marks are excluded; unattributed records carry no actor weight.
  3. Panel Judge Mirror. Known judges are replicated as mirror personas built only from receipted signals — every preference claim cites its signal or is struck. Mirror scores are recorded forecasts, graded person-level only where identity and signals are receipt-established, venue-archetype otherwise. Every run reports its dispersion; a run that cannot discriminate files a null verdict on itself.
  4. G0 exit test. A milestone closes only when its full chain fires — record of record, settlement stamp, independent replay, and an overseer's exit check — with zero-due legs (payouts, escrow) named as zero-due rather than silently passed.
  5. Pre-registered campaign design. Acceptance thresholds are fixed before execution and never edited after: the Speculators campaign of record admits at accuracy ≥ 0.50, speedup ≥ 1.20, margin ≥ 0.10, behind a lossless gate (any output mismatch disqualifies). At the epoch's close the campaign stands at 0 of 48 runs executed — stated, not rounded up.
  6. MVT (multivariate testing). Nested under DoE, never a peer plane. Its epoch product of record is the pool-scope method: per-category shares as stamped snapshots with statistical bounds, and an evidence floor — below n = 30 in the window, no point estimate is published at all.
  7. Snapshot method (Amendment 6). A percentage is lawful only as a snapshot stamped {at · method · bounds} derived from a recorded allocation rule. A constant percentage anywhere is a defect; an unrecorded rule renders NOT_ESTABLISHED.
  8. QUE-plane admission and ordering as treatments. The queue's admission and ordering decisions are themselves experimental treatments, run over the instrumentation of record; a controlled comparison of orderings is NOT_ESTABLISHED this epoch and is stated as such.

The cycle, in plain words

Every cycle starts by writing down what will be tested, before anything is built (preregister). No skipping this. Then: build it, check it, get a second independent check, decide the verdict, and write the result into the record. After recording, there are two ways forward: add a new layer — which sends the cycle back to "check it" again — or close the cycle out. "Close" is final; nothing leaves the closed state.

The eight stages: preregister → build → verify → corroborate → verdict → register → {layer | close}, with layer → {verify | close} the only other moves — nine allowed transitions in total, exactly as the proof packet E001 defines them.

What the picture does not show: when a move is allowed, only that it is allowed. Going back to "check it" is meant to happen only when something materially changed, and the second check must really be a second check, not the same one repeated. The picture gives you the map; the text gives you the traffic rules.

Larger shapes nest: generation ⊂ iteration ⊂ cycle ⊂ epoch — an epoch is one full pass over the dataset of record; a generation is one turnover of a population under evaluation and culling.
